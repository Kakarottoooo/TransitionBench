What this case establishes—and what it does not

  • It supports an accounting correction: service before declared warm-up completion must count.
  • Keeping A produced zero qualified completions. First positive gain is not repayment of an observed negative balance; percentage uplift would be misleading.
  • The advice tied immediate switching. WAIT at 20 seconds did not avoid a measured loss. Already running B is a different initial condition.
  • These are three nested windows of one synthetic workload, not three scenarios. Three seeds are not a confidence guarantee.
  • APC is an existing vLLM optimization. This is not evidence of StateAware superiority or Wafer production gains.
Why the simpler strategy matters

In an earlier, separate 36-trial study, immediate switching completed 239–253 more qualified requests than a cost-aware rule under sustained traffic. Those runs are not pooled with this case and are not included in the downloadable ZIP; this separate result is an author-reported summary. Later state-aware research did not establish an advantage over strong simple baselines.
